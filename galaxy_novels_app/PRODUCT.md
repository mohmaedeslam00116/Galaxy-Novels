# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Galaxy Novels is for Arabic readers who browse, follow, and read serialized novels on mobile. The main context is leisure reading on phones, often in short sessions, with frequent movement between home updates, library search, novel details, history, and the native reader.

## Product Purpose

The app turns the Galaxy Novels website data into a smooth native Android reading experience. Success means users can find novels, inspect details, continue reading, and read chapters without feeling that the app is a wrapped website or a heavy visual experiment.

## Brand Personality

Dark, refined, readable. The interface should feel modern and richer than a plain utility app, while staying calm enough for long reading sessions.

## Anti-references

Do not make the UI look like a generic card grid, a WebView shell, a gradient-heavy anime app, or a decorative landing page. Avoid crowded cards, nested cards, heavy animation, blurred/glass effects, beige/brown dominance, and visual tricks that slow scrolling.

## Design Principles

1. Reading comes first: every visual decision must protect legibility and calm focus.
2. Covers carry the richness: use real novel covers and stable poster/list dimensions instead of decorative backgrounds.
3. Navigation stays familiar: Material 3 navigation, predictable back behavior, and simple page structure.
4. Rich, not noisy: use deep navy surfaces, electric blue states, and restrained gold highlights without gradients.
5. Future themes are planned: all color decisions route through theme tokens so a theme picker can be added later.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Support RTL Arabic layout, readable line height, clear touch targets, visible feedback on taps, reduced unnecessary motion, and reader settings that let users adjust font size, line height, and reading palette.
